# Cancelling or changing your plan

## Upgrading

You can upgrade from Free to Pro, or from Pro to Team, at any time in Settings > Billing > Change plan. Upgrades take effect immediately. You pay a prorated amount for the rest of the current billing period.

## Downgrading

Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period. If you downgrade to Free and use more than 1 GB of storage, your existing notes stay available but you cannot add new attachments until you are under the limit.

## Switching between monthly and annual billing

Switching from monthly to annual takes effect immediately and you are charged the annual price minus any unused time. Switching from annual to monthly takes effect when your annual period ends.

## Cancelling

Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, then your account moves to the Free plan. Your notes are never deleted when you cancel. To delete your notes, you need to delete your account.
