# Billing and payments

## Accepted payment methods

CloudNotes accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. In India, UPI is also accepted for annual plans. We do not accept cheques or bank transfers, except for Team plans with 50 or more users, which can be invoiced.

## When you are charged

Monthly plans are charged on the same day each month as the day you subscribed. Annual plans are charged once a year on the anniversary of your subscription. You will receive an email receipt for every charge.

## Updating your payment method

Go to Settings > Billing > Payment method and select Update. The new card is used for your next charge. If a payment fails, we retry three times over 7 days. If all retries fail, your account moves to the Free plan, and nothing is deleted.

## Invoices

Every invoice is available in Settings > Billing > Invoices, where you can download it as a PDF. Invoice numbers start with INV- followed by six digits, for example INV-204518. To add a company name or tax ID to future invoices, enter it under Settings > Billing > Billing details.

## Taxes

Prices exclude sales tax, VAT and GST. Tax is calculated from your billing address and shown on your invoice. Customers in India are charged 18% GST.
