# Security and privacy

## Encryption

All notes are encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Pro and Team users can additionally protect individual notes with a password, which encrypts the note end to end. CloudNotes cannot recover a password-protected note if you forget its password.

## Where data is stored

Data is stored in data centres in the United States and the European Union. Team plans can choose EU-only storage when the team is created.

## Privacy

CloudNotes never sells your data and does not use the content of your notes to train AI models. Support staff can only open your notes if you give permission in a support ticket.

## Compliance

CloudNotes is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. Team customers can request a data processing agreement (DPA) from support.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report security issues to security@cloudnotes.example. We respond within 2 business days.
