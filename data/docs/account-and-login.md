# Account and login

## Resetting your password

On the login screen, select Forgot password and enter your email address. We send a reset link that expires after 1 hour. If the email does not arrive within 10 minutes, check your spam folder. Accounts created with Google or Apple sign-in do not have a CloudNotes password; use the same sign-in button instead.

## Changing your email address

Go to Settings > Account > Email. We send a confirmation link to the new address, and the change takes effect once you click it. Team plan members must ask their admin to change their email.

## Two-factor authentication

Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) in Settings > Security. CloudNotes supports authenticator apps such as Google Authenticator and Authy, and hardware security keys. When you enable 2FA you get 10 backup codes; store them somewhere safe. If you lose your 2FA device and your backup codes, contact support. For your security, account recovery takes 3 business days and requires identity verification.

## Locked accounts

After 10 failed login attempts, an account is locked for 30 minutes. Wait 30 minutes or reset your password to unlock it.
