# Sync troubleshooting

CloudNotes syncs automatically whenever you are online. A grey cloud icon means a note is waiting to sync, and a green check means it is synced.

## Notes are not syncing

1. Check that you are signed in with the same account on every device.
2. Check your internet connection, then pull down on the notes list (mobile) or press Ctrl+R / Cmd+R (desktop) to force a sync.
3. Update the app to the latest version. Versions older than 6 months can no longer sync.
4. On the Free plan, check that you are not using more than 2 devices. A third device will not sync until you remove one in Settings > Devices.
5. Sign out and sign back in. Unsynced notes are kept on the device and upload after you sign back in.

## Sync conflicts

If the same note is edited on two devices while one is offline, CloudNotes keeps both versions. The second copy is named with "(conflict)" and the device name, so you can merge them by hand.

## Sync is slow

Large attachments sync after text, so text appears first. Syncing more than 1,000 notes for the first time can take up to 30 minutes. If sync stays slow, check the status page at status.cloudnotes.example for any ongoing incident.
