# Offline use and mobile apps

## Apps

CloudNotes is available on Windows 10 and later, macOS 13 and later, iOS 16 and later, Android 10 and later, and in any modern web browser at app.cloudnotes.example.

## Offline access

The desktop apps keep all your notes available offline on every plan. On mobile, notes you opened recently are cached, but full offline notebooks are a Pro and Team feature. To make a notebook available offline on mobile, open the notebook menu and turn on Available offline.

## Changes made offline

Edits made offline are saved on your device and sync automatically the next time you are online. See Sync troubleshooting if a note shows "(conflict)".
