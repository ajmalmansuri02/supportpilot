# Refund policy

## Duplicate or incorrect charges

If you were charged twice for the same period, or charged after cancelling, you get a full refund of the extra charge. Duplicate charges are usually caused by a payment being retried while the first attempt was still processing. Contact support with the invoice numbers and we will refund the duplicate. Refunds reach your card in 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

## Annual plans

Annual Pro and Team subscriptions can be refunded in full within 30 days of the charge. After 30 days, annual plans are not refunded, but you keep access until the end of the paid year.

## Monthly plans

Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months. When you cancel, you keep access until the end of the month you paid for.

## How to request a refund

Open a support ticket from the app (Help > Contact support) or ask the support assistant. Include the email address on your account and the invoice number. Refunds above $100 must be approved by a billing specialist, which can take up to 2 business days.
