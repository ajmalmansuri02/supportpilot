# Deleting your account and data

## Deleting your account

Go to Settings > Account > Delete account and confirm with your password. Deleting your account cancels any subscription and permanently deletes all notes, notebooks and attachments.

Deleted accounts can be restored within 14 days by contacting support. After 14 days, all data is permanently erased from our servers, and backups are erased within 30 more days.

## Before you delete

We recommend exporting your notes first. See Exporting your notes.

## Team accounts

Members of a Team plan cannot delete their own account. Ask your team admin to remove you. Notes in shared team spaces belong to the team and stay after a member is removed.
