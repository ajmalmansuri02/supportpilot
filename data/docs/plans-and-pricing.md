# Plans and pricing

CloudNotes has three plans: Free, Pro and Team. Prices are in US dollars and exclude local taxes.

## Free plan

The Free plan costs $0 and includes:

- Up to 2 devices synced to one account
- 1 GB of storage for notes and attachments
- Attachments up to 10 MB each
- 30 days of version history
- Sharing notes with view-only links

## Pro plan

The Pro plan costs $8 per month, or $80 per year when billed annually (two months free). Pro includes everything in Free, plus:

- Unlimited devices
- 100 GB of storage
- Attachments up to 250 MB each
- 1 year of version history
- Offline notebooks on mobile
- PDF and Word export
- Priority email support with a reply within 24 hours

## Team plan

The Team plan costs $15 per user per month, or $150 per user per year when billed annually. The minimum is 3 users. Team includes everything in Pro, plus:

- 1 TB of pooled storage per team
- Shared team spaces with edit permissions
- Admin console with user management
- Single sign-on (SSO) with Google Workspace and Microsoft Entra ID
- Unlimited version history
- Live chat support during business hours

## Discounts

Students and teachers get 50% off Pro with a valid school email address. Registered non-profit organisations get 30% off the Team plan. Discounts cannot be combined.
