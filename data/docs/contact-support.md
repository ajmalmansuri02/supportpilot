# Contacting support

## Support channels and response times

- Free plan: help centre and the support assistant. Email support replies within 3 business days.
- Pro plan: priority email support with a reply within 24 hours, 7 days a week.
- Team plan: priority email plus live chat, Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 in your team's chosen time zone (UTC, US Eastern or India Standard Time).

## Talking to a person

The support assistant can hand your conversation to a human agent at any time. Just ask for a person. Billing disputes, account recovery and data-loss problems are always handled by a human.

## Service status

Check status.cloudnotes.example for outages and planned maintenance. Planned maintenance happens on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC.
