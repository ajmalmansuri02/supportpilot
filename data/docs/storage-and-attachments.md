# Storage and attachments

## Storage limits

Free accounts have 1 GB, Pro accounts have 100 GB, and Team plans have 1 TB of storage pooled across the team. Text notes use very little space; attachments such as images, PDFs and audio make up most storage. See your usage in Settings > Storage.

## Attachment size limits

A single attachment can be up to 10 MB on Free and 250 MB on Pro and Team.

## When you reach your limit

When you reach your storage limit, you can still create and edit text notes, but you cannot add new attachments. Free up space by deleting attachments or emptying the Trash, or upgrade your plan.

## Trash

Deleted notes stay in the Trash for 30 days and still count towards your storage. Empty the Trash to free space immediately.
